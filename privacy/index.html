Privacy policy.

Effective September 19, 2026. Token Widget is operated by Austin Stephens.

What the app does

Token Widget connects to your eligible Codex account to display usage summaries and optionally generate pixel-art companions. Preview mode uses sample data and does not connect an account.

Account connection and usage

You authenticate on OpenAI’s website. We do not receive your OpenAI password. Our Railway-hosted service stores authorization credentials, account email and plan information, session identifiers, and cached usage summaries, including available allowance, reset information and reported token statistics. Service records are encrypted at rest. The device stores its service token in the iOS Keychain and shares usage snapshots with its widget.

Creature creation

When you generate a companion, your description is sent through our service to OpenAI using your connected account. Generation consumes your Codex allowance or credits. The service stores a fingerprint of the description, request status, generated pixel art, and any reported generation token counts. A temporary isolated worker processes the request and is removed after it ends. Descriptions and drafts can also be saved locally so you can resume. OpenAI’s handling and retention of submitted content are governed by your OpenAI account settings and its policies.

What we do not collect

The app does not retrieve your conversation-history text, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or precise location. We do not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics, sell personal data, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Creature prompts are the text you explicitly submit, not imported conversation history.

Retention and deletion

Service sessions expire 30 days after creation and are removed by the service’s expiry cleanup. Disconnect account deletes that service connection, credentials, cached usage and generation results. If cleanup cannot finish offline, the app retains the information needed to retry and displays that state. Deletion from Token Widget does not delete data separately retained by OpenAI or delete your OpenAI account. Local companion artwork, settings and creation drafts may remain on your device until removed with the app; device backups are managed by your device settings.

Service providers and support

Railway hosts the connection service. OpenAI provides authentication, account usage and requested generation. GitHub hosts this website and support issues. Network infrastructure receives ordinary connection metadata, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Public GitHub support issues are visible to others; do not post secrets or personal account information.

Your choices

You can use preview mode, choose whether to generate a companion, disconnect each device’s service connection, and manage your OpenAI account directly. For support or privacy questions, see the support page. Policy changes will be published here with a revised effective date.
